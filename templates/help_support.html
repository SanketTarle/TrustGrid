Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a worker?

Go to Find Worker, choose a verified worker, select your preferred date & time and confirm your booking.

How can I cancel a booking?

Open My Bookings, choose your booking and click Cancel Booking before the scheduled time.

How do refunds work?

Eligible refunds are processed within 3–7 business days directly to your original payment method.

How can I contact customer support?

Use Live Chat, email support or raise a support ticket from this page.
